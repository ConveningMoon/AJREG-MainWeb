# /hogar — family affordability calculator (Persuade, mobile-first, ES default)

Scope: lead magnet for Adriana at the "Carpa Familiar" family event (2026-10-11), Latina families standing at the booth with a phone, outdoors in daylight. Success: a family finishes 4 questions, leaves name + email, reads their number and chats with Adriana. Campaign-scoped world: does not change global tokens. Terms are email-only news, non-promotional; no text/WhatsApp is sent by the team (the WhatsApp button is user-initiated).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page IS the carpa: a hand-painted mercado sign under a striped awning. Refuses the cream-page / serif / gold-accent real-estate calculator and the generic quiz card stack.
OWN-WORLD: Drenched marigold field (#f7b500) with navy ink (A&J navy #102037), rosa-mexicano pink (#d6246e) for actions and chosen states, turquoise (#0a7a76) for support, cream (#fff7f5) sign boards framed by a navy pinstripe border. Heavy sign-painter display face (Bowlby One) for headlines and prices, Montserrat for everything readable. Striped scalloped awning at the top, a bunting string of papel-picado flags as the progress meter, hanging price tags for the range, an authored casita illustration.
STORY: A family sees a friendly painted sign, understands in one line what they get ("tu número real, 60 segundos"), taps through four big questions, and gets their price painted on the sign with a burst of confetti, then chats with Adriana.
