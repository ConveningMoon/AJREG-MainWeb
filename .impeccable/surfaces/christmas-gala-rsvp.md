# Christmas Gala — private guest RSVP

Audience: personally invited adults; complimentary attendance. EN/ES. Share by
direct link; noindex, no sitemap or primary navigation. This is distribution
privacy, not an authenticated invitation system.

Confirmed: December 5, 2026, 7–11 PM Eastern, The Royal Courtyard at West Neck;
semi-formal attire, adults only, no RSVP deadline. One primary attendee and at
most one adult companion. No companion names, payment or real estate questions.

Direction: evergreen night invitation with a white RSVP card on mobile and
the original full cream form column on desktop. Shared
brand fonts, gold hairline frame, existing pine sprigs and wine ornaments.
Desktop full-width two-column composition, without a centered outer width cap;
fills at least the viewport height. Mobile uses one continuous evergreen surface,
with introduction, free-standing event details, then a white form card inset
within the same evergreen background. Desktop places details below the
introduction beside the full cream form column. Mobile form padding is
2.25rem × 1.5rem with 1rem corners; form title/subtitle are centered.
Paired name fields and
44px minimum inputs. Site navbar, utility bar and footer are hidden only when
this RSVP is present; an independent EN/ES switch remains in the invitation.
Facts and form
remain text, independent of decoration. Existing canvas snowfall is dynamically
loaded and static with reduced motion. LazyMotion animates form interactions.

The logo links to `/`. Event details have no enclosing border or separate fill:
date, time and venue occupy one row at every breakpoint. The venue name itself
links to the map with a 44px minimum tap target; there is no directions button.
The full address sits beneath the venue name in the same third column.
Adults-only and semi-formal attire sit as
centered inline captions with Users/Shirt icons beneath the address, without
individual borders or backgrounds resembling buttons. All event-block content
is centered, including date/time labels, venue and address.

Opening experience: a cream paper envelope covers the entire viewport on each
fresh load, with layered folds, evergreen sprigs and a wine/gold A&J wax seal.
The seal is a native button: click/tap/Enter/Space detaches the seal, opens the
flap in perspective, lifts a real separate letter layer out of the pocket and
slides the envelope down before a final short dissolve. LazyMotion sequences
these layers over ~1.5 seconds. A seeded, tiled SVG noise texture covers the
paper folds and inner letter, rather than sitting behind opaque layers.
Content mounts behind the opening envelope and stays inert/aria-hidden until
the animation ends, then receives heading focus. Root scrolling is locked only
while the envelope is present. Reduced motion uses a brief fade without spatial
movement. EN/ES and the home-linked
logo are available on the envelope. No storage or autoplay audio is added.
Without JavaScript, event facts remain readable with an enable-JS message;
the form is hidden to prevent accidental native submission.

Social previews: localized 1200×630 JPEG invitations, cream/gold/evergreen with
pine branches, ornaments and the existing logo. Only guest-facing copy, event
date/time/venue and private complimentary admission; no sponsor prices/deadline.
Generated by `scripts/og/christmas-gala-sponsors.py --audience rsvp` using brand
fonts from the Next cache. OG/Twitter use absolute, versioned image URLs. Public
previews become available after deployment; no live publishing is authorized.

Conversion: name/email, optional surname/phone, clear 0/1 companion radio choice,
live total including the primary attendee. ITMANO success is required before
confirmation. Duplicate registrations receive separate copy; failures retain
input. No advertising consent per explicit user instruction.
